# Northwind Enterprise Employee Leave Policy (2026 Edition)

## 1. Annual Paid Time Off (PTO)
All full-time employees accrue 20 days of paid vacation per calendar year, accrued at the rate of 1.67 days per full month worked.
A maximum of 5 unused PTO days may be carried over into the subsequent calendar year, expiring on March 31st.

## 2. Sick and Medical Leave
Employees receive 10 paid sick leave days annually, renewed on January 1st.
Absences exceeding 3 consecutive business days require formal documentation from a licensed healthcare provider submitted to People Operations.

## 3. Parental Leave
Primary caregivers are eligible for 16 weeks of fully paid parental leave following the birth, adoption, or foster placement of a child.
Secondary caregivers are eligible for 8 weeks of fully paid leave.

## 4. Bereavement and Compassionate Leave
Up to 5 days of paid bereavement leave is granted for immediate family members, and up to 3 days for extended family.

## 5. Request and Approval Process
All planned leave requests must be logged through the internal HR Portal at least two weeks in advance and approved by the reporting manager.
Emergency or unplanned medical leave must be reported via notification to the team lead before 09:00 AM on the day of absence.
